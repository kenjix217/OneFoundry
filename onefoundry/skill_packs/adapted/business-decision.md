# business decision memo

Use this procedure to compare options for a stated product or business decision using metrics and context supplied in the work order or approved memory.

1. State the decision, owner, deadline, options, success measure, and non-negotiable constraints. Ask for an omitted option if it could materially change the choice.
2. Compare options on the same dimensions. Show the actual supplied figures, time periods, denominators, and sources; identify incompatible comparisons.
3. Explain likely effects, costs, risks, and reversibility. Label an interpretation as an inference when the data does not directly prove cause.
4. Recommend one option only if the evidence distinguishes it. Otherwise say what experiment or data would resolve the choice, with a bounded next step.
5. Return a short recommendation, evidence table, key uncertainty, and decision checkpoint.

Do not imply that OneFoundry queried a live system, approved spend, or executed the decision.
