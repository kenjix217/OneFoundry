# deep research intake

Use when a broad question needs multiple source passes and a cited document deliverable. Inputs: research question, audience, time and geography bounds, source classes, required claims, and desired DOCX structure.

1. Plan discovery and focused contradiction or gap checks.
2. Build a claim-to-source ledger from actual admitted research results, distinguishing source facts and inference.
3. Outline a document with claims, caveats, and citations for later authoring and page review.

Capability preflight: For research reads require admitted Researcher public-web tools and configured search; for final DOCX require a native document authoring and rendering path with visual review. OneFoundry lacks DOCX creation and visual QA.

Output: Return a bounded research plan or source-backed text dossier plus a DOCX blocker; never label text as the required completed report. Save a requested Markdown brief only when `write_text_artifact_v1` is admitted.
