# capital market window brief

Use this limited market-window update for a named issuer and possible financing. It supports a preliminary prepare, defer, or investigate posture from supplied terms and dated public evidence; it does not execute or recommend a transaction launch.

1. Record issuer, public or private status, funding need, use of proceeds, timing constraint, instrument under consideration, and the exact source cutoff. Do not assume a target size or security.
2. Separate issuer facts from current market observations. An admitted built-in Researcher may fetch public HTTPS sources without disclosing private financing plans in queries; absent live rates, spreads, price, book feedback, or ratings views must remain explicit gaps.
3. Compare the stated instrument with a small set of plausible alternatives. Name issuer-specific launch conditions, no-go triggers, disclosure or covenant questions, and facts needed for a banker or counsel decision.
4. Return a focused status and immediate workplan with sourced facts and assumptions labeled. Save Markdown through `write_text_artifact_v1` only when requested and admitted.

Do not claim investor demand, legal eligibility, securities-law clearance, financing terms, or a live executable market window.
