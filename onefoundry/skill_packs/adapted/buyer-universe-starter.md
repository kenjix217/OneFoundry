# preliminary buyer universe

Use this starter procedure when the operator names a company, asset, or financing need and asks for possible buyer, sponsor, lender, or investor categories. An admitted built-in Researcher may verify public candidate facts without placing confidential mandate details into public queries; no private relationship or mandate system is connected.

1. Define seller or issuer objective, asset perimeter, geography, size, exclusions, and the candidate type being screened. Keep strategic, sponsor, lender, and investor logic separate.
2. Include a named candidate only with a cited public business fit or operator-supplied relationship fact. Label capacity, mandate, conflicts, regulatory fit, and interest as unknown unless verified.
3. Rank a small preliminary research queue by fit and disqualifying question. Explain why each candidate is on the list and which fact could remove it.
4. Return a dated starter list, source pointers, exclusions, and a verification plan. Save text or CSV with `write_text_artifact_v1` only when requested and admitted.

This is not a vetted buyer book, an outreach list, evidence of interest, or permission to contact anyone.
