# public issuer baseline

Use this quick text procedure for a named listed issuer when the operator needs factual context before deeper research. An admitted built-in Researcher may use `public_web_search_v1` and then `public_page_fetch_v1` for official public sources; without them, work only from supplied material and identify missing current evidence.

1. Confirm the legal issuer, ticker and exchange when sourced, reporting period, requested lens, and as-of cutoff. Do not confuse a similarly named company, security, or subsidiary with the issuer.
2. Summarize its business and relevant segments from a dated filing or company disclosure. Include only decision-useful reported metrics with period, unit, currency, source URL, and confidence. Separate company claims from observed numbers and analyst inference.
3. Add recent disclosed events, material risks, and evidence gaps that change the baseline. Use `decimal_calculate_v1` for bounded arithmetic only when its inputs and compatible periods are explicit. Do not infer current valuation, consensus, ownership, or a price target from stale or missing market data.
4. Return a compact baseline and the next research question it enables. Save Markdown through `write_text_artifact_v1` only when admitted and requested.

This is a public-source profile, not private diligence, a model, a recommendation, or a verified live market-data snapshot.
