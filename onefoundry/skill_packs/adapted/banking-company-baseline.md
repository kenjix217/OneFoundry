# banking company baseline

Use this preliminary profile for a named target, borrower, issuer, or counterparty in a banker-owned question. It may use supplied facts and public pages fetched by an admitted built-in Researcher; private-company facts require operator-supplied evidence. Keep confidential mandate and deal facts out of public queries.

1. Confirm legal entity, role in the transaction, perimeter, ownership status if sourced, and as-of date. Keep parent, subsidiary, asset, and operating company distinct.
2. Summarize business model, scale, segments, capital structure, and transaction-relevant risks only where evidence supports them. Label seller statements and sponsor claims as claims.
3. Report each financial number with source, period, currency, accounting basis, and any adjustment. Do not infer enterprise value, debt capacity, or management quality from missing data.
4. Return a compact baseline, contradictions, and the minimum source request for the next banker decision. Save Markdown only when `write_text_artifact_v1` is requested and admitted.

This is a screening profile, not a diligence pack, valuation, or verified borrower underwriting.
