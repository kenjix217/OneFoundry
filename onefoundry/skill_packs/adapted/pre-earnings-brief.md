# short pre-earnings brief

Use this procedure only for an explicitly requested short or quick preview **before** a named public issuer reports. The built-in Researcher may search and fetch public HTTPS sources if `public_web_search_v1` and `public_page_fetch_v1` are admitted; supplied estimates or notes remain labeled by their actual owner and timestamp.

1. Confirm issuer, fiscal period, announced reporting date or window, and a source freeze time. If timing is unconfirmed, say so. Do not mix results from another quarter or company.
2. Establish the latest reported baseline and any publicly stated guidance. Add a consensus or other expectation bar only when a dated, named source was actually supplied or read. Keep GAAP and adjusted values, units, and currencies separate.
3. Name the two or three key debates and the metric or management answer that could resolve each one. Draft concise public-information call questions. Include likely evidence gaps, especially missing consensus, options, price, or transcript access.
4. Return a short source-linked setup with cutoff, bar, debates, questions, and what remains unknown. A scenario is conditional, not a forecast. Save Markdown with `write_text_artifact_v1` only when admitted and requested.

This does not create the source workflow's full HTML report or provide a trade-ready position instruction. Do not claim results, a market reaction, or an earnings surprise before the release.
