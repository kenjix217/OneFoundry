# content calendar draft

Use this procedure to propose a short content plan from a supplied campaign goal, audience, channel choices, dates, and approved messages.

1. Name the desired audience action and the evidence behind each message. Preserve any brand, claim, privacy, or approval constraints in the work order.
2. Choose a small number of content items that serve distinct purposes. Avoid repeating the same claim across channels without a reason.
3. For each item, give a proposed date, channel, working title, message, source evidence, owner, and draft status. Mark a date as tentative when no schedule was supplied.
4. Identify dependencies, review points, and missing assets. Do not invent a launch date or claim that an asset exists.
5. Return a compact calendar table and the next operator decisions.

This is a planning draft. OneFoundry does not schedule a recurring run, publish a post, or send a message from this procedure.
