# banking meeting brief

Use this limited procedure for an explicitly requested transaction, lender, issuer, or sponsor meeting brief. Work from supplied mandate and participant facts; an admitted built-in Researcher may use public web evidence for public facts only.

1. Identify the deal or financing question, meeting purpose, participants and roles as actually supplied, information cutoff, and decision or follow-up expected.
2. Separate known transaction facts, public background, unverified claims, and confidential internal-only questions. Record the intended circulation mode and avoid-saying items. Never put private deal names, terms, people, or documents into public web queries.
3. Draft a short public-information agenda and prioritized questions that test the largest value, financing, diligence, or process uncertainty. Keep nonpublic diligence questions in a separate internal section until the operator confirms the permitted disclosure and NDA channel. Give the source or reason for each question and the answer that would change the next step.
4. Return a one-page preparation note with open items and a debrief capture prompt. Save Markdown through `write_text_artifact_v1` only when requested and admitted.

This brief does not contact participants, read a private data room, or imply that an NDA, relationship, or invitation exists.
