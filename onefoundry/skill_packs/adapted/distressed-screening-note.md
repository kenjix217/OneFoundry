# distressed screening note

Use this preliminary note for a named distressed company, claim, or restructuring question when the operator asks for a screen from thin or public evidence. Keep confidential case facts out of public queries. Do not call it a recovery waterfall unless operative priorities, claims, liens, and values are supplied.

1. Identify debtor and relevant legal entities, security or claim class, jurisdiction if sourced, process stage, evidence cutoff, and whose decision the note supports.
2. List known debt, cash, maturity, collateral, and process facts with their source. Separate reported terms, public filings, operator assumptions, and unknown intercreditor or priority terms.
3. State likely gating questions for liquidity, claim validity, seniority, collateral, enterprise value, and scenario timing. Calculate a simple supplied ratio only through admitted `decimal_calculate_v1` and show the inputs.
4. Return a screening posture, major uncertainty, source request, and next diligence step. Save Markdown only when `write_text_artifact_v1` is requested and admitted.

No recoveries, fulcrum call, legal priority conclusion, creditor instruction, or board recommendation follows from missing operative documents.
