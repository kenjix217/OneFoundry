# Codex finance and data pack coverage

These are **OneFoundry-authored, limited procedures**, not copies of Codex plugins or complete replacements for their runtimes. An installed library version does not grant a role tools, source access, or permission to send data. The live text route uses saved and current tool admission; public search/fetch is limited to the built-in Researcher with configured access. All finance outputs remain preliminary when prices, consensus, holdings, operative documents, or verified private records are missing.

## Data Analytics

Installed concepts: `analyze-data-quality` → supplied-data review; `design-kpis` → KPI design; `gather-business-context` → context framing; `kpi-reporting` → supplied KPI readout; `market-sizing` → market sizing; `metric-diagnostics` → supplied metric diagnosis; `product-business-analysis` → business decision memo; `validate-data` → supplied analysis review; `share-artifact-summary` → supplied artifact summary. The last one summarizes exact operator-supplied content for internal copying; it cannot read an arbitrary saved artifact or share it externally.

Deferred: `build-dashboard`, `build-report`, `visualize-data`, and `index` need the reviewed Data app, source access, rendering, and visual verification; `convert-to-doc`, `convert-to-slides`, and `report-to-pdf` need document export and inspection; `jupyter-notebooks` needs notebook file and execution tools; `create-data-context` needs a durable context owner; `publish-artifact-to-sites` needs publication and readback; `schedule-refresh-jobs` needs a scheduler and repeatable source reads.

## Public Equity Investing

Installed concepts: `catalyst-calendar` → public catalyst brief; `company-tearsheet` → public issuer baseline; `earnings-deep-dive` → short post-earnings brief; `earnings-preview` → short pre-earnings brief; `idea-generation` → public equity idea triage; `meeting-prep` → public company meeting brief; `comps-valuation` → public peer comps screen; `economic-impact-report` → public equity shock brief; `memo-builder` → public equity memo update; `scenario-sensitivity-generator` → public equity scenario screen; `thesis-tracker` → public equity thesis update. The last five are bounded screens or short updates and cannot produce the source pack's full models, report app, or portfolio actions.

Deferred: `dcf-model-builder`, `equity-model-update`, `financials-normalizer`, `model-audit-tieout`, and `three-statement-model-builder` need spreadsheet ingestion, formula inspection, recalculation, and export; `deck-report-qc` needs rendered artifact inspection; `event-driven-analyzer`, `initiating-coverage`, `long-short-pitch`, and `portfolio-risk-management` need stronger current market, portfolio, event-math, or execution evidence for their full decision contracts; `user-context` needs durable preference and source-pointer ownership; `public-equity-investing` is a router, not a standalone analysis procedure.

## Investment Banking

Installed concepts: `buyer-investor-list` → preliminary buyer universe; `capital-markets-issuance` → capital market window brief; `company-tearsheet` → banking company baseline; `comps-valuation` → banking quick comps review; `distressed-recovery-waterfall` → distressed screening note; `meeting-prep` → banking meeting brief; `private-credit-underwriting` → initial private credit screen. Each is confined to an explicit starter, quick, or screening mode. No candidate interest, market access, legal priority, credit approval, or transaction readiness is implied.

Deferred: `cim-builder`, `cim-teardown`, `covenant-package-analyzer`, and `financials-normalizer` need controlled deal documents and source-to-claim or schedule review; `dcf-model-builder`, `lbo-model-build`, `merger-model-builder`, `model-audit-tieout`, `scenario-sensitivity-generator`, and `three-statement-model-builder` need formula workbooks and verified calculation/export tools; `deal-process-tracker` needs a persistent process tracker and controlled source updates; `ib-deck-qc` and `pitch-deck-builder` need native/rendered deck tools; `memo-builder` needs a controlled source packet and circulation artifact; `user-context` needs durable preference ownership; `investment-banking` is a router, not a standalone banker workflow.
